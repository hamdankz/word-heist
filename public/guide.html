How to play

Find words. Attack rivals. Survive.

01 / FIND A WORD

Search the shared grid

Every board has 10 hidden target words, each 4–10 letters long. Drag or tap the first and last letter. Words run horizontally, vertically or diagonally, in either direction.

Try tapping the P and T in PLANET.

First finder claims the word in their player colour. Claimed letters may still be used in overlapping words. Accidental words aren't targets.

02 / BUILD POWER

Longer words hit harder

Keep your streak

Find consecutive words before anyone else. Each claim doubles your multiplier. Streaks survive new boards and skip votes, but a rival claim breaks them.

03 / CHOOSE A REWARD

Attack or recover

⚔ Attack

Choose a living opponent. Damage = (letters − 3) × streak multiplier × round multiplier.

♥ Recover

Restore one heart. If already at full health, gain a shield instead. Each shield blocks one incoming damage. Rewards left unchosen for 20 seconds automatically recover.

All words found, or every surviving player votes to skip? Outstanding rewards resolve, then solutions appear for four seconds and a new grid loads. Health, shields and streaks carry over.

Win the match

Zero hearts means elimination. Last surviving player or team wins. The final leaderboard shows words, damage and best streak.
